# Accessibility Statement

We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to continually improve the user experience and apply relevant accessibility standards across our repositories and applications.

## Standards & Conformance

We aim to align our projects with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA** standards.

Key principles we prioritize include:
- **Keyboard Operability**: Ensuring interactive components can be navigated and operated using a keyboard alone.
- **Screen Reader Compatibility**: Structuring content semantically with clear headings, landmarks, and text alternatives.
- **Visual Contrast**: Maintaining readable text contrast ratios and avoiding relying solely on color to convey meaning.

## Reporting Accessibility Barriers

If you encounter accessibility barriers in any of our repositories or project documentation, please let us know:

1. **GitHub Issues**: Open an issue in the relevant repository and apply the `accessibility` label.
2. **Direct Contact**: Email [joshbruce](https://github.com/joshbruce) with details regarding the barrier encountered.

Please include:
- The URL, file, or component where the issue occurred.
- A brief description of the barrier and any assistive technology used.

We aim to provide an initial assessment within **72 hours** and apply remediations promptly.
